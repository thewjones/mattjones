### Braggoscope {#braggoscope}

<https://www.braggoscope.com>

In Our Time is a long running live Radio 4 programme where Melvyn Bragg discusses the history of ideas with a panel of 3 experts.

With an archive of over 1000 episodes it's an incredible resource of information but the BBC's online facilities to search the archive are somewhat limited.

Thankfully [Matt Webb](https://interconnected.org/) has provided [such a tool](https://www.braggoscope.com) by scraping the BBC's archive and making a browseable and searcheable index using the [Dewey Decimal System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dewey_Decimal_Classification).

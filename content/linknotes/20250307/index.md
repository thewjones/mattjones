### Some Wrexham roads to raise speed limits to 30mph {#some-wrexham-roads-to-raise-speed-limits-to-30mph}

<https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c3vp4l1qe10o>

Progress is hard. Mainly because those who shout the loudest get their way. Mass motorisation brought about a mass delusion that roads were built for cars. They weren’t, they were built for everyone going about their daily business on any form of transport or none. Residential streets were built for children to play on, as much as anything else.

When drivers can only see the purpose of roads from their perspective, a speed limit reduction from 30mph to 20mph seems like an affront. It's a failure to recognise the benefits of a drop in speed limit to everyone apart from themselves.
